Brand activations — common questions

What brand teams and agency producers ask before requesting a quote.

How much does a floral brand activation cost?+

Activation packages start at $1,315 for 25 bouquets, and larger events are custom quoted. Every package includes professionally processed flowers and a styled floral display, two attendants for two hours, company-branded bouquet wrapping, bouquet bags and ribbon, and delivery, setup and breakdown. A branded backdrop or logo is quoted with your event.

Do you serve both Los Angeles and Orange County?+

Yes. We bring floral brand activations to venues across Los Angeles County and Orange County, from retail and shopping-center spaces to offices, hotels and pop-up locations. Delivery, setup and breakdown are included, and some farther locations may include a travel fee, confirmed in your quote.

Do guests pay for their bouquets?+

No. The brand or agency books the activation, and guests build their bouquets at no cost.

Can guests receive pre-made bouquets instead?+

Yes. Guests can create their own bouquets with guidance from our attendants, or receive pre-made bouquets prepared by our team. Let us know which you prefer in your quote request.

Can the activation be branded?+

Yes. Every package includes company-branded bouquet wrapping, and we can tailor the flower palette to your brand colors. A branded backdrop or logo display is quoted with your event.

How much lead time do you need?+

Two to four weeks is the standard lead time. Custom-branded bouquet wrapping also needs time for artwork approval and printing, so we recommend sharing your logo files early in your planning.

Can you provide a Certificate of Insurance?+

Bring the Bloom Bar & Rentals carries general liability insurance and can provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) upon request.
