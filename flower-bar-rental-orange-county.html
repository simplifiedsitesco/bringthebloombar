Flower Bar Rental Questions

Answers to what hosts ask most before booking.

How far do you travel for a flower bar rental?+

We’re based in La Habra and travel up to 30 miles for full-service events, which covers most of Orange County plus nearby LA County and San Bernardino County cities. Full-service experiences have a $110–$125 fee that covers delivery, design, setup and breakdown.

How many guests can a flower bar rental serve?+

Full-service flower bar rentals start at 15 guests and are priced per guest, so the flowers are scaled to your guest list. For 35 or more guests, contact us directly so we can plan the setup with you.

How long does flower bar setup take?+

We arrive about 1 hour before your event to set up, so the flower bar is styled and ready before your first guest walks in.

What happens to leftover flowers after the event?+

Any leftover flowers stay with you, so you can enjoy them after the party.

How do I reserve a flower bar rental?+

Book online: choose the Full Bloom Bar Experience, pick your collection and color palette, and add your event details. A $100 deposit reserves your date, and we confirm availability within 24 hours.
