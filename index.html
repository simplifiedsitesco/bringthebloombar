What Is a Bloom Bar Rental?

A bloom bar is a styled flower station you rent for your event — guests walk up, choose their favorite stems, and build their own bouquet to take home. It works as both décor and entertainment, giving your shower, birthday, wedding, or corporate event a hands-on floral moment that feels intentional and memorable.

We offer two ways to experience it. With our DIY rentals starting at $49/day, you bring the flowers and we bring the beautifully styled bar — setup and breakdown included. Prefer a fully flowers-included experience? Our full flower packages start at $725 and include everything from sourcing to styling to delivery.

Either way, your guests leave with something they made themselves.

Questions about renting a bloom bar

Do you provide flowers?+

Yes — we offer both. Our DIY rentals start at $49/day and you bring your own flowers. If you prefer a fully flowers-included experience, our full flower packages start at $725 and include sourcing, arranging, and delivery. Contact us to learn more about flower package availability.

Can I book a flower bar rental near me?+

Yes, we serve cities across Orange County and beyond for showers, birthdays, weddings, backyard parties, and corporate events. See all locations.

What if I only need one rental item?+

Yes — our Flower Bar stand is available on its own starting at $49/day. You can also add a boho umbrella with our Boho Bloom Bundle at $84/day. Both are bookable directly through our online calendar.

How far ahead should I reserve?+

Two to four weeks is ideal, especially in spring and early summer weekends.

What events is a bloom bar perfect for?+

Our bloom bar works beautifully for bridal showers, baby showers, birthday parties, weddings, wedding receptions, corporate events, engagement parties, quinceañeras, and backyard parties.

How much does delivery cost?+

Delivery and setup starts at $14 for cities within our local service area. For cities further out, delivery is calculated at $14 plus $1.50 per mile. Contact us for an exact quote for your city.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Cancellations more than 14 days before your event may receive a refund minus any deposit. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit all deposits. Cancellations within 7 days may be charged the full rental amount.
