What Is a DIY Bloom Bar?

A DIY bloom bar is a self-serve flower station you rent for your event — no florist required. Guests walk up, choose their favorite stems, and build their own mini bouquet to take home. It works as both décor and entertainment, giving your shower, birthday, or backyard party a hands-on floral moment that feels intentional and memorable. Starting at $49/day, it’s one of the most affordable ways to make your event feel elevated without full-service floral pricing.
