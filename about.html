Our Story

Every event deserves a moment guests actually talk about.

That’s why we created Bring the Bloom Bar.

Parties have a lot of moving parts — the food, the decorations, the logistics. But the moments guests remember aren’t the centerpieces. They’re the experiences they participated in.

Bring the Bloom Bar was built around one simple idea: give every guest something to do, something to create, and something to take home.

I’m Loreen — a local educator turned entrepreneur with over 20 years of experience working with people and curating environments where they feel engaged and welcomed. When I made my way into the party and event space, I saw the same gap everywhere: events that looked beautiful but felt passive. Guests standing around, drinks in hand, waiting for the next thing to happen.

A bloom bar changes that. Guests gather around, pick their stems, build their bouquets, and leave with something they made themselves. It becomes the activity and the favor — built in.

We launched in 2026 with a clear mission: make this experience accessible to everyone. That’s why we offer two ways to bring the bloom bar to your event. Rent the bar from $49/day and bring your own blooms — or let us handle everything, flowers included, from $725. Whether you’re a maid of honor planning something unforgettable for the bride, a company investing in a real experience for your team, or simply a host who wants to give your guests something more — we have an option for you.

Transparency is at the core of how we operate. Clear pricing, honest timelines, and a commitment to showing up and delivering exactly what we promise.

We’re just getting started — and we can’t wait to bring the blooms to your next event.

— Loreen, Founder
