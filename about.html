Our Story

Beautiful Events Shouldn’t Require a Floral Designer Budget

Bring The BloomBar started with one simple belief: moms and hosts deserve a gorgeous party moment without spending thousands on professionally arranged florals. A DIY flower bar gives your event style, interaction, and personality — all while staying in control of your budget.

We built this service for people who love creativity. You choose flowers that fit your theme, your colors, and your spending comfort. We provide the key pieces that make the setup look polished and photo-ready.

Why DIY bloom bars work

Instead of paying for pre-made centerpieces, you let guests create something personal. It becomes an activity and a keepsake at the same time.

Why clients trust us

Our pricing is clear, our rental options are simple, and our style is made for modern showers, birthdays, and backyard events across Orange County.
