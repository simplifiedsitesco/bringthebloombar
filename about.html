Our Story

Beautiful Events Without the Floral Designer Price Tag

Your guests deserve a stunning, hands-on experience — without you spending thousands to make it happen.

We started Bring the Bloom Bar because we believed one thing: you shouldn’t have to choose between a gorgeous event and staying on budget. A DIY flower bar gives your guests something to do, something to talk about, and something to remember — all at a fraction of the cost of traditional florals.

You pick the flowers that match your theme and colors. We deliver the stand, table, linen, umbrella, easel, and accessories — fully set up and ready for your guests. You just enjoy the day.

Built for bridal showers, baby showers, birthdays, weddings, backyard parties, and corporate events across Orange County, Los Angeles County, and the Inland Empire.
