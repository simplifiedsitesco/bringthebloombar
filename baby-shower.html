Baby Shower Flower Bar — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a bouquet bar for your baby shower.

What is a baby shower flower bar?+

A flower bar is a styled floral station where baby shower guests pick their own stems and arrange a custom take-home bouquet. Bring the Bloom Bar provides all flowers, setup, and breakdown — serving Orange County and surrounding areas including parts of LA and San Bernardino County.

How many guests do you need for a baby shower flower bar?+

Our Full Bloom Bar Experience has a 15-guest minimum. For smaller baby showers, our DIY rental option has no guest minimum — you bring the blooms and we bring the styled bar.

Can a flower bar work for an outdoor baby shower?+

Yes — our bloom bar setup works beautifully both indoors and outdoors. We bring everything needed for the display and can accommodate most venue types, from backyard patios to indoor event spaces.

How far ahead should I book a flower bar for a baby shower?+

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks in advance. Fresh flowers are sourced within 48 hours of your event date to ensure peak freshness. Spring and summer weekends fill quickly, so earlier is always better.
