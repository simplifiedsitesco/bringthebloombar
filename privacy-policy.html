Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or services, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide to us, such as when you submit an inquiry or booking request, use our website chat, make a payment, or contact us directly. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Event date, address, and location
  • Guest count, service preferences, and rental details
  • Messages you send us and related communication history
  • Payment information (collected securely by Stripe)

When you visit our website, certain information is also collected automatically through analytics tools, such as your IP address, browser and device information, approximate location, pages visited, and how you interact with the website. See Section 5 for details.

We do not collect sensitive personal data directly through our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Process and manage rental reservations
  • Process payments
  • Improve our website and understand how visitors use our services
  • Communicate with you about your rental or inquiry

We do not sell or share personal information with marketers or unrelated third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate trusted third-party services to operate our website and deliver services. These may include:

  • Booqable — used for rental management and reservations
  • Stripe — used for secure payment processing
  • Google Analytics — used to understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Microsoft Clarity — used to understand how visitors interact with our website through heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity may collect information such as mouse movements, clicks, and scrolling behavior. You can learn more at: privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
  • Customer relationship management and automation service providers — used to organize inquiries, maintain customer records, and follow up with customers
  • AI service providers — used to generate automated responses, including through our website chat

These services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect certain technical information necessary for their services.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Communications

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals may use artificial intelligence (AI), virtual assistants, customer relationship management platforms, and automated communication systems to respond to customer inquiries, provide information about services, assist with preliminary quotes, recommend packages, and guide prospective customers through the booking process.

These systems may communicate through text messages, email, website forms, and other supported communication channels. Some responses may be generated automatically without individual human review.

To provide relevant customer service, these systems may process customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, event dates, event locations, guest counts, service preferences, and relevant communication history.

Automated systems may use this information to recommend appropriate services, provide preliminary pricing based on established business rates, follow up with prospective customers, and direct customers to appropriate booking or payment processes.

Automated lead-management systems are not intended to independently modify or cancel existing reservations, issue refunds, or access full payment card details or sensitive payment credentials.

We may use third-party customer relationship management and automation service providers and AI service providers to process information on our behalf. Such processing may include storing customer records, maintaining communication history, generating responses, and supporting customer service operations.

We do not authorize service providers to use customer information to train generalized public AI models on our behalf. Information handling, storage, retention, and permitted uses are subject to applicable provider agreements and service configurations.

Automated systems may associate communications with an existing customer record using a phone number or email address. Matching contact information does not independently verify a person’s identity.

Customers may request assistance from a human representative at any time, subject to normal business hours and availability.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Online Tracking

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our website, evaluate website performance, and improve our services.

These services may collect information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, approximate geographic location, pages visited, and website interactions, including clicks, scrolling behavior, and session recordings.

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity may use cookies or similar technologies to collect this information. These third-party providers may process information in accordance with their applicable privacy policies and service agreements.

We do not currently use advertising pixels or remarketing technologies to deliver targeted advertisements to website visitors.

You may manage or restrict certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

Do Not Track: Our website does not currently respond to browser-based Do Not Track signals. Certain browser privacy settings may nevertheless restrict tracking technologies. Where legally required, we will honor applicable privacy preference signals.

For additional information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement

6. Data Retention

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to inquiries, managing customer relationships, providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, and applicable legal requirements.

When personal information is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes, we will delete, deidentify, or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements and permitted exceptions.

Customers may contact us at lriley@bringthebloombar.com to request information about their personal data or exercise applicable privacy rights.

7. Your Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request information about how their data is used

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals does not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us at: lriley@bringthebloombar.com

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 13.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. SMS and Text Messaging

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals may collect and use customer telephone numbers to communicate regarding inquiries, quotes, event planning, booking assistance, and related customer service matters.

When customers provide appropriate consent, communications may include automated or AI-assisted text messages and related follow-ups.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Customers may opt out of text messages by replying STOP or request assistance by replying HELP. We also honor other legally recognized requests to withdraw consent. You can also contact us at (714) 251-6478 or lriley@bringthebloombar.com.

Providing consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing services or submitting an inquiry.

Consent to receive inquiry-related communications does not automatically authorize unrelated promotional or marketing messages.

We may maintain records of consent, including consent status, submission date, source form, and applicable consent disclosure, for compliance and recordkeeping purposes.

Mobile telephone numbers and SMS consent information are not sold or shared with third parties for their independent marketing purposes. SMS consent information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be provided to service providers as necessary to deliver authorized communications.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by the revised Effective Date. Your continued use of the website after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
