Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or services, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information you voluntarily provide to us, such as when you submit a booking request, make a payment, or contact us directly. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Event address
  • Rental details
  • Payment information (collected securely by Stripe)

We do not collect sensitive personal data directly through our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Process and manage rental reservations
  • Process payments
  • Improve our website and understand how visitors use our services
  • Communicate with you about your rental or inquiry

We do not sell or share personal information with marketers or unrelated third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate trusted third-party services to operate our website and deliver services. These may include:

  • Booqable — used for rental management and reservations
  • Stripe — used for secure payment processing
  • Google Analytics — used to understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Microsoft Clarity — used to understand how visitors interact with our website through heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity may collect information such as mouse movements, clicks, and scrolling behavior. You can learn more at: privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

These services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect certain technical information necessary for their services.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar tracking technologies through tools such as Google Analytics to better understand how visitors use our website. These tools may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on the website, device type, and general geographic region. You may disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer not to allow this type of tracking.

5. Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics to better understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics does not provide us with personally identifiable information about visitors. You can learn more at: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to fulfill services, respond to inquiries, or comply with legal requirements. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at lriley@bringthebloombar.com.

7. Your Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request information about how their data is used

Bring the Bloom Bar and Rentals does not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us at: lriley@bringthebloombar.com

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 13.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by the revised Effective Date. Your continued use of the website after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us: lriley@bringthebloombar.com
