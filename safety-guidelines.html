Safety

Event Rental Safety Guidelines

Customer assumes full responsibility for the safe use, supervision, and monitoring of all rented equipment during the rental period.

Adult supervision is required at all times when children are using or near any rented equipment or games. Children should never be left unattended around rental equipment. Equipment use should be discontinued immediately if unsafe conditions arise, including unstable surfaces, rough play, unsafe weather, damaged equipment, overcrowding, or improper use.

Giant Jenga Safety Guidelines

  • Use only on flat, stable surfaces.
  • Adult supervision is required when children are present.
  • Do not climb, sit, lean, or stand on the tower.
  • Players and guests should stand clear when the tower becomes unstable.
  • Falling blocks may cause finger, hand, foot, or toe injuries.
  • Avoid rough play, pushing, or unsafe stacking.
  • Do not use during strong winds or unsafe outdoor conditions.
  • Discontinue use immediately if the tower becomes unstable or damaged.
  • AGE WARNING: Not recommended for children under 10 years of age per manufacturer safety guidelines.

Main Risks: Falling blocks, pinched or smashed fingers, foot or toe injuries, injuries caused by climbing or misuse.

Cornhole Safety Guidelines

  • Use in open areas away from walkways, vehicles, or crowded spaces.
  • Players should remain aware of throwing direction at all times.
  • Guests should not stand in front of active throwers.
  • Boards should remain on level and stable ground.
  • Adult supervision is required when children are present.
  • Do not engage in rough or reckless play.

Main Risks: Bean bags striking guests, trips and falls, slips near game areas.

Ring Toss Safety Guidelines

  • Rings should be tossed gently and responsibly.
  • Maintain safe spacing between players and spectators.
  • Adult supervision is required when children are present.
  • Ensure the game base remains stable throughout play.
  • Do not throw rings aggressively toward guests or faces.

Main Risks: Rings striking eyes or faces, trips or falls around game pieces.

Giant Connect 4 Safety Guidelines

  • Use only on flat, stable ground.
  • Do not climb, hang, lean, or sit on the frame.
  • Keep fingers clear of the release slider and moving parts.
  • Adult supervision is required during play when children are present.
  • Avoid rough play, pushing, or tipping of the game structure.
  • Stop use immediately if the frame becomes unstable or damaged.
  • CHOKING HAZARD: Contains small parts. Not recommended for children under 3 years of age.
  • Do not allow small children to place game pieces in their mouths.

Main Risks: Tipping hazards, pinched fingers, choking hazards for children under 3, frame damage or collapse caused by misuse.

Umbrellas, Flower Bars, Tables & Event Displays

  • Umbrellas must remain secured and should be closed immediately during strong wind or unsafe weather conditions.
  • Flower bars, tables, and display items must remain on stable and level surfaces.
  • Do not lean, climb, sit, or hang on display structures or tables.
  • Customer is responsible for monitoring weather, wind, ground conditions, and guest interaction throughout the rental period.
  • Company is not responsible for damage or injuries caused by weather, wind, improper setup conditions, or misuse after delivery.

Main Risks: Wind-related tip-over, collapse or instability, property damage, guest injuries.
