Bridal Shower Flower Bar — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a bouquet bar for the bride-to-be.

What is a bridal shower flower bar?+

A bridal shower flower bar is a styled floral station where guests pick their own stems and build a custom take-home bouquet — a hands-on activity and a beautiful party favor in one. Bring the Bloom Bar handles all flowers, setup, and breakdown, serving Orange County and surrounding areas including parts of LA and San Bernardino County.

How many guests do you need for a bridal shower flower bar?+

Our Full Bloom Bar Experience has a 15-guest minimum. For smaller bridal showers, our DIY rental option has no guest minimum — you bring the blooms and we provide the styled bar and cart.

Can the flower bar be customized to match our bridal shower theme?+

Yes — we work with you to select a color palette that complements your event. Whether you’re going for soft romantic whites, garden-style botanicals, or a specific color scheme, we source fresh blooms within 48 hours of your event to match.

How far in advance should I book a flower bar for a bridal shower?+

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks in advance. Weekend spring and summer dates fill quickly, so earlier is always better. A $100 deposit holds your date once you book.
