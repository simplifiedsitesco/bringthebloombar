A Closer Look at the Flower Bar

Questions from Maids of Honor

What the bridal party usually asks before booking.

Can you match the bride’s colors or shower theme?+

Yes. You choose a color palette when you book, and we source fresh flowers within 48 hours of the shower to match. Tell us about the bride’s colors or theme and we’ll help you pick the palette that suits it best.

Does it work at a restaurant or someone’s home?+

Yes, it works beautifully in both. We set up at homes, in backyards and on restaurant patios. If the shower is at a restaurant or venue, please check with them about space and any vendor rules before you book.

How early do you arrive to set up for the shower?+

We arrive about 1 hour before guests do, so everything is styled and ready when the bride walks in. We handle breakdown after the shower, and any leftover flowers stay with the host.

We’re expecting a big group. How many shower guests can you handle?+

Collections start at 16 guests and are priced per guest, so the flowers scale with your list. If you’re expecting 35 or more, contact us directly and we’ll plan the setup with you.

How do we reserve the date for the bride?+

Book online, choose your collection and color palette, and add your shower details. A $100 deposit reserves your date, and we confirm availability within 24 hours.
